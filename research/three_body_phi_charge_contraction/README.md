# Three-body \(M+\Phi''\) vector-charge contraction

**Status:** matched charge-sector coefficient; zero-momentum form-factor
benchmark; PR #53 three-body \(I_{00}\) kernels combined with PR #54 AFH
Xe-131 responses. This is not the complete all-current natural-xenon rate.

## Physical matching

The one-nucleon vector charge in Hoferichter--Menéndez--Schwenk,
PRD 102, 074018 (2020), Eq. (41), contains both the leading \(M\) term and
the spin-orbit \(\Phi''\) term. With \(q_A=k'-k\), \(t_A=-|\boldsymbol q_A|^2\),
and AFH Fourier momentum \(\boldsymbol q_{\rm AFH}=-\boldsymbol q_A\),
\[
c_M=F_1^V(t_A)+\frac{t_A}{8m_N^2}(F_1^V+2F_2^V),
\qquad
c_{\Phi''}=\frac{|\boldsymbol q_A|^2}{4m_N^2}(F_1^V+2F_2^V).
\]
For the radiative process, both operators multiply the exact three-body
time current \(\mathcal T_3^0\). The common phase-space factor cancels from
the relative charge-sector correction:
\[
R_{\rm charge/M}=
(1-\eta/2)^2+
2(1-\eta/2)\eta\frac{W_{M\Phi''}}{W_M}
+\eta^2\frac{W_{\Phi''}}{W_M},
\quad
\eta=\frac{q_A^2}{4m_N^2}\left(1+2F_2^V/F_1^V\right).
\]

The spacelike components do not contribute to the \(M\) and spin-orbit
\(\Phi^{00}\) charge responses in the multipole decomposition discussed
immediately below Eq. (41) of Hoferichter--Menéndez--Schwenk. Spatial
current terms remain necessary for other responses, including
\(\Delta,\Sigma',\Sigma''\).

## Adopted zero-momentum benchmark

Use the project flavour benchmark
\[
F_1^V(0)=3,\qquad F_2^V(0)=-0.552,\qquad F_2^V/F_1^V=-0.184,
\]
with \(m_N=0.938918\) GeV. This is not a finite-\(t_A\) form-factor fit.

At the exact second Xe-131 \(W_M\) minimum,
\[
q_A=256.271867617\ {\rm MeV},\quad E_R=269.103897622\ {\rm keV},
\]
the matched charge response is
\[
R_{\rm charge/M}=0.9700938740,
\]
a \(-2.9906\%\) correction relative to the leading \(F_1^V(0)^2W_M\)
baseline. This includes the \(M\)-coefficient Darwin--Foldy term,
\(M\)-\(\Phi''\) interference, and \(\Phi''\)-squared term.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs:
- PR #53 three-body current table:
  research/vector_current_response_audit/results/2026-11-18/vector_current_response_audit.csv
- PR #54 AFH response grid and exact minima snapshots under input/.

Outputs generated by the workflow:
- phi_charge_contraction_grid.csv: 2401 AFH momentum points and all
  \(F_2/F_1\) sensitivity scenarios.
- phi_charge_contraction_three_body_benchmarks.csv: PR #53 \(I_{00}\)
  kernels corrected in the charge sector for all width/g2 benchmarks.
- phi_charge_contraction_exact_afh_minima.csv: exact selected AFH minima.
- summary.json and SHA256SUMS.txt: summary and provenance.

The output uses local order-6/order-8 interpolation for the PR #53 momenta
and retains exact AFH selected points for the minima. It checks the AFH
Cauchy--Schwarz bound and the positivity of the matched charge response.

## Limitations

- The adopted \(-0.184\) ratio is a zero-momentum benchmark. Finite-\(t_A\)
  flavour-separated form factors remain necessary.
- The calculation includes the charge-sector \(M,\Phi'',M\Phi''\) responses,
  not the spatial-current \(\Delta,\Sigma',\Sigma''\) responses.
- Xe-131 is not a substitute for the other naturally abundant isotopes.
- The native Mathematica cross-check in PR #54 remains pending.
- No complete natural-xenon spectrum, detector convolution, likelihood,
  or manuscript-level result is claimed.
