# LZ detector-response inputs and rate-level archive

This directory stores the LZ 2026 detector-efficiency input and the
rate-level folds that connect pre-detector natural-xenon recoil spectra to
the published high-efficiency nuclear-recoil window.

## Inputs

- lz_2026_extended_nr/lz_2026_figS2.svg: retained vector source figure.
- lz_2026_extended_nr/lz_2026_nr_efficiency_digitized.csv: 303-point
  digitization of the central final WIMP-search ROI efficiency and gray
  uncertainty envelope.
- lz_2026_extended_nr/lz_2026_nr_efficiency_metadata.json: source,
  extraction method, axis calibration, checks, and digitization caveats.

## Rate-level result archives

- lz_2026_extended_nr/rate_level_event_yields/: earlier GCN/JJ55 continuous
  spectrum efficiency folds.
- lz_2026_extended_nr/afh7_hybrid9_rate_level_yields/: AFH-7 and Hybrid-9
  continuous spectrum folds, including CSV, JSON provenance and SHA-256
  manifest. Four benchmarks times two scenarios give eight output rows.

The AFH-7/Hybrid-9 fold is generated by
research/figure2_full_reproduction/fold_lz_2026_afh_hybrid9_rate_level.py.
Its regression tests are in
research/figure2_full_reproduction/tests/test_lz_2026_afh_hybrid9_rate_level_fold.py.
The end-to-end workflow passed in
[GitHub Actions run #43527099172](https://github.com/lm846802297-bit/LZ-3-body-for-hdm/actions/runs/43527099172).

## Provenance and interpretation

The efficiency curve is digitized from LZ Collaboration, arXiv:2609.02823v1,
Fig. S2 (PDF page 14). The corresponding HEPData release is
[doi:10.17182/hepdata.182472.v1](https://doi.org/10.17182/hepdata.182472.v1).
The metadata records the PDF SHA-256, vector-path extraction procedure, and
comparison with an independent digitization.

The gray band is retained as a detector-systematic range. LZ does not assign
a confidence level to that envelope. The curve is not rescaled to force exact
agreement with the rounded published mean efficiency.

AFH-7 uses official AFH v6.0 default-density responses for the seven supported
isotopes. Xe-124 and Xe-126 are omitted without renormalizing the other
abundances. Hybrid-9 uses Helm only for those two missing isotope responses;
it is a labelled diagnostic fallback, not a full nine-isotope AFH result.
Neither fold recomputes the AFH nuclear responses or the archived three-body
kernel.

All archives are rate-level diagnostics only. They do not include the
two-dimensional S1c/log10(S2c) signal PDFs, NEST nuisance covariance,
energy-resolution smearing, background likelihood, or profile likelihood.
They are not a 90% confidence-level exclusion and are not the official LZ
likelihood.
